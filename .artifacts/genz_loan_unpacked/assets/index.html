Terms & Privacy

1. Global Service Agreement

Welcome to GenZ Loan Professional. By accessing this platform, you enter into a legally binding agreement with GenZ Fintech Solutions. You certify that you are at least 18 years of age and possess the legal capacity to enter into financial contracts in your jurisdiction.

2. Data Privacy & Biometric Security

We employ 256-bit AES encryption to protect your data. By proceeding, you grant GenZ Loan the right to collect and process biometric data (facial recognition), device information, and financial records solely for the purpose of identity verification and credit scoring. We comply with international data protection standards (GDPR/NDPR).

3. Loan Terms & Dynamic Interest

Interest rates are determined by our proprietary AI algorithm based on your credit profile, ranging from 8.5% to 15.2% APR. Repayment periods vary between 21 and 120 days. All loan offers are subject to final verification of documents.

4. International Loan Disbursal

Loans are disbursed in your local currency based on international conversion rates at the time of transaction. A non-refundable technical processing fee (equivalent to 500 KES/approx $4 USD) is required to facilitate secure cross-border payment gateway integration and credit insurance.

5. Default & Credit Reporting

Failure to repay within the stipulated period will result in a daily penalty of 1% on the outstanding balance. Defaulted accounts will be reported to international credit bureaus, potentially affecting your future borrowing capacity globally.

6. Anti-Fraud Policy

Submission of forged documents, use of VPNs to spoof locations, or providing false employment data constitutes financial fraud. GenZ Loan reserves the right to initiate legal proceedings and permanently ban fraudulent accounts from the network.

7. Governing Law

These terms are governed by international financial arbitration standards. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding digital arbitration in accordance with the rules of the International Chamber of Commerce.
